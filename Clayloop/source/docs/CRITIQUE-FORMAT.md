# Portable or installation critique?

**Recommendation: installation-based critique (Dec 10), with the portable critique (Dec 7–8) as a fallback that ClayLoop fully supports.**

## Why
The thesis claim lives in the *relationship* between a physical material and its digital mirror. Critics need to see clay, camera and
screen side by side, and ideally to nudge real clay themselves and watch the form respond. On a laptop alone, ClayLoop collapses into a
screen-based replay, which is exactly the "story about the artifact" the project argues against.

The hardware does not make an installation hard:

| Item | Needed for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| MacBook Pro (Apple Silicon) | everything | runs fully offline once installed |
| External monitor (24–32") | exhibition display | HDMI/USB-C; run Exhibition Mode on it full-screen |
| USB webcam on a small tripod/clamp | live mode | side view at the height of the clay, about 40–70 cm away |
| Banding wheel (hand turntable) + a lump of clay | live mode without a pottery wheel | a cheap, portable stand-in: off-centre clay sways, centred clay doesn't |
| Plain dark backdrop card behind the clay | segmentation reliability | about A2 size; matte |
| Small lamp, diffuse | stable lighting | venue lighting varies; avoid lights that flicker |
| Table about 120 × 60 cm, 1 power outlet | — | no water, no wheel, no network |
| Physical clay sample(s) | material presence | e.g. a centred and an un-centred lump |

No pottery wheel, no internet and no developer tools are required. If the camera fails on the day, Exhibition Mode's documented session
still works. Make sure that session is a **real** recording before Dec 4 (see below).

## When portable would be the better choice
- The installation slot cannot give you a table, an outlet and a monitor.
- You have **no real recorded session** by early December. Then the honest presentation is a smaller, laptop-based demo of the system
  using the synthetic session, clearly labelled, rather than an installation that implies empirical data.

## What you need to do, by deadline
- **Nov 13–17:** run ClayLoop on the MacBook with the external webcam at a real wheel. Calibrate, measure the noise floor, and record 3–5 real
  centering sessions (your own and, with consent, a peer or instructor). Tell me what breaks.
- **Nov 18 (presentation type form):** choose *installation*.
- **Nov 19 (catalog content):** use screenshots from Exhibition Mode, ideally from a real session (`docs/screenshots/` has placeholders made from
  synthetic data; label them as such if you use them).
- **Nov 30 (requirements form):** request the table, the outlet and a monitor (or bring your own), and wall or backdrop space. No network.
- **By Dec 4:** mark your best real session **Use in exhibition** in the Library, and rehearse the guided replay.
- **Dec 9 (setup):** calibrate on site under the venue's lighting. Use `ClayLoop-Exhibition.command`.
